Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

SwipeX uses this website to explain a statement review for merchants in and around San Bernardino, California, and to let you request that review.

What this page collects

This page does not include a form that sends information to SwipeX. The cost calculator runs in your browser. The sales and rate figures you type stay on your device and are not sent to SwipeX.

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to count page visits and to record when someone clicks a review button or changes the calculator. Google may set a cookie to tell a new visit from a return visit. The sales and rate figures you type are not sent to Google Analytics. This website does not run advertising tags.

Booking

“Review My Processing Costs” and “Review My Costs” open a Calendly scheduling page: https://calendly.com/vicbarrios24/30min. Anything you submit there, such as your name, email, and the booking questions on that page, is collected by Calendly and shared with SwipeX so the review can be scheduled. Calendly's privacy policy applies to that page.

SwipeX uses booking details to schedule and hold the 30-minute statement review. This website does not sell information, because it does not take a form submission of its own.

Statements

A merchant statement is not uploaded on this website. If you bring a statement to the review, that happens off this page.

Questions

This website does not publish a phone number or an email address. Questions about information you entered while booking go through the Calendly page for that booking.

The rules for using this website are on the Terms page.
