Can a business pass credit card fees to customers in California?

California has long restricted credit card surcharges under Civil Code §1748.1. Merchants may still offer discounts for cash or other non-credit payments when those discounts are available to all prospective buyers. Dual pricing and cash-discount setups have extra rules, and advertising rules matter too. This is general information, not legal advice. SwipeX can talk through options that may fit your business during a statement review.

What is dual pricing or a cash discount program?

Dual pricing means posting different prices for cash or debit versus credit. A cash discount program usually posts a card price and offers a lower price for cash or other non-credit payments. Merchants use these to offset processing costs. Setup and disclosures must follow card-network and state rules.
